Frequently Asked Questions

Navigate to the Home page and enter your departure point in the "From" input and your destination in the "To" input. Tap Find Available Buses to view scheduled buses, current occupancy levels, available seats, and departure timings.
Click the Use GPS button right above the "From" input on the Home page. Your browser will prompt you for location permission. Once granted, BusMitra detects your coordinates and automatically fills your nearest bus station or landmark.
Tap the blue microphone button inside the "To" destination input box. Speak clearly (for example: "Guntur", "Mangalagiri", or "Tenali"). The Web Speech API will transcribe your spoken word directly into the field. This is designed especially for elderly passengers and commuters on the move.
Available seats are calculated dynamically:
Available Seats = Maximum(0, Total Bus Capacity - Current Passengers).
If a bus with a 50-passenger capacity currently carries 38 passengers, exactly 12 seats remain available. If standees cause passenger count to exceed 50, available seats display 0 and the bus is flagged as OVERCROWDED.
Our hardware setup uses dual-beam infrared (IR) beam-break sensors paired with an ESP32 microcontroller at the entrance and exit doors. When a commuter passes through, the sequence in which the two infrared beams are broken tells the controller whether someone entered (IN count +1) or exited (OUT count +1). The difference provides real-time onboard passenger density.
While IR sensors count physical entry and exit in real time, they cannot know each passenger's destination. Next Stop Prediction combines current onboard numbers with historical stop boarding/alighting rates to forecast whether you will get a seat by the time the bus reaches your stop.
